Modular Airport Terminal

Winter 2025

A concept for a terminal combining retail, dining, and transit into one compact footprint

Type Self-guided Study
Tools Rhino, Illustrator, Photoshop, D5

Design Concept

Inspired by the complexity of airports with their many different crucial functions, this concept for a terminal combines efficient circulation with retail and dining. All three modules contain the central elevated “highway” featuring two moving walkways and a pathway in the middle for accessibility transport. One module features four stairways and an elevator to allow people to get form one level to the other. The other two modules have four gates along the sides with shops and restaurant spaces below the walkway. There is a version with three different stores and another with one large space.
