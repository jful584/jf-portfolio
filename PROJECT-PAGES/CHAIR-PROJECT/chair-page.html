Reconfigurable Furniture

Spring 2026

A seat that transforms by rotating and re-arranging its modular parts

Type First Year Group Studio Project
Tools Rhino, Illustrator, Photoshop, CNC Router

Precedent Study

The design and concepts for this project were inspired by Shigeru Ban. His creative use of renewable materials such as paper and humanitarian efforts pushed our group to design a piece of furniture that could be used for several tasks and could be easily stored and transported. We took many ideas from cardboard bed, most notably the idea of using interlocking panels that could be easily assembled and stored.

The Design

Starting from the cardboard bed and the logic of interlocking panels, our group worked to create a design that could not only be easily assembled, but also re-assembled into other configurations. To manufacture the piece, we were limited to one 2' x 4' piece of 3/16" thick plywood. Therefore, we had to allow each piece be able to be used in several different ways. By making the panels able to slide into different positions and by allowing the furniture to stand in different orientations, we were able to transform the bed into a seat, table, shelf, and more. Finally, the long planks were added to support a mattress, act as hangars, provide back support, and more.
